# QATION: Quantum AI Platform

QATION is India's first fully-integrated Quantum-AI research and development platform. Built to bridge the gap between classical Generative AI and Quantum Mechanics, it provides a seamless workspace for researchers, students, and engineers.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Unified Workspace
- **Workspace View:** Write a prompt and get working Qiskit or PennyLane circuits instantly. The circuits are executed and drawn live, gate-by-gate, right in the browser. 
- **Real Datasets:** Built-in loader for actual molecular datasets (LiH, H₂, QML benchmarks) so you can run real simulations instead of just generating dummy code.

### 2. General AI & Quantum Context
- **General Text Generation:** Fully supports general reasoning, essay writing, and conversational chat—just like ChatGPT or other popular LLMs.
- **Human-Like AI Writing (ZeroGPT Evasion):** Outputs are dynamically structured to avoid known AI markers (e.g., words like "delve", "tapestry"), ensuring high burstiness and perplexity to evade AI detectors.
- **Q-SIL Context Tracking:** Powered by our proprietary Quantum Structural Interaction Layer (Q-SIL). A deterministic 2-qubit circuit evaluates structural linguistic invariants in sub-milliseconds to completely prevent hallucination and context bleed.

### 3. Advanced Tools
- **Quantum Code Explainer:** Paste any quantum code (Qiskit/PennyLane) or classical code (Python/JS) to receive a plain-English, gate-by-gate or step-by-step breakdown.
- **Research Paper Summarizer:** Upload a quantum computing PDF and get key insights extracted automatically.
- **Collaborative Notebooks:** Google Docs-style real-time collaborative quantum notebooks to share your research natively.
- **Public Circuit Gallery:** A community-submitted gallery of circuits with upvotes and comments.

### 4. Tracking & Benchmarks
- **Usage Dashboard:** A visual dashboard charting your queries run, circuits simulated, and tokens used.
- **Quantum Benchmark Tracker:** Track and compare simulation results across different backend configurations over time.
